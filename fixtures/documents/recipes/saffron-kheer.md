# Saffron kheer

Ingredients: 1 litre of full-fat milk, 80 grams of basmati rice, 12 saffron threads, 100 grams of sugar,
and chopped pistachios to garnish.

Soak the rice for 20 minutes. Simmer it in the milk on low heat for 45 minutes, stirring often.
Add the sugar and the saffron (soaked in warm milk) in the last 10 minutes. Serve chilled.
