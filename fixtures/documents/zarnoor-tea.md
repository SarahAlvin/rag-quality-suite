# Zarnoor Tea Co.

Zarnoor Tea Co. is a family tea company founded in 1987 in Murree by Nasreen Qadir.
It started as a single stall near the Mall Road and now employs 42 people.

In 2001 the company moved its head office to Abbottabad to be closer to its warehouse.

The signature blend is called Silver Pine, a green tea with dried pine needles and a hint of cardamom.
A second blend, Monsoon Ember, is a smoky black tea sold only between July and September.
